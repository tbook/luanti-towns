# Stock village templates: attribution

`tavern.mts`, `well.mts`, `library.mts` and `blacksmith.mts` are derived from
the VoxeLibre `mcl_villages` schematics `tavern.mts`, `well.mts`,
`library.mts` and `blacksmith.mts` (VoxeLibre 0.92.3).

- Author: MysticTempest ("Completely new schematics for VoxeLibre")
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC-BY-SA 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Changes: the tavern's two fence-and-plate tables are replaced with tables,
  chairs and plates as Living Villages does at village generation; the
  library's wooden stair in the middle of the back bookshelf wall is replaced
  with a lectern, also as Living Villages does; the buried dirt layers are
  dropped except the top dirt row, which is kept as the ground row; every
  building is rotated so its door or opening faces -Z and empty outer rows
  and columns are trimmed. The well is unchanged apart from being captured
  with its full apron box. The blacksmith is unchanged apart from rotation
  and trimming.

See `tools/workshop_export/README.md` ("Stock village templates") for how the
files are regenerated.
